# Triage of the 704 waiting test files, 18 Nov 2026

Every row of `parity/tests.tsv` with status `waiting` was sorted into one pile; the piles are in
`TRIAGE-2026-11-18.tsv` (columns `file`, `pile`, `rust`, `why`). `parity/tests.tsv` was not changed.

Who sorted what: Gemini 4 Hydrogen sorted `shared`, `preload`, `packages/`, `apps/desktop/test` and the first 322 files
of `main` (366 rows). Its workflow ran out of quota partway through `main`. Claude Opus 5.5 sorted the last 48 `main`
files and all 290 `renderer` files (338 rows), and spot-checked Gemini's rows: 11 of its `have-it` functions were
sampled, 10 existed as named and one was corrected (`agents.rs::probe_definition` to `discover_one`). Items marked "Checked" in
the list below were re-read in the Rust source for this note.

## What to do with it

- **have-it (178): the cheap wins.** Rust already does the behaviour; port the test and name the Rust function in
  `tests.tsv`. Nothing to build.
- **does-not-apply (106): close them.** They pin React, the browser DOM, Electron IPC, Windows-only paths or TypeScript
  source shapes. None needs a Rust test.
- **feature-missing (85): park them** with their row (mostly voice, row 10, and phone, cloud and chat apps, row 12).
  They come back when the row is built.
- **duplicate (5) and low-value (1): close them** once the Mac chat agrees with the reason.
- **gap (329): this is the real backlog**, and it is a backlog of behaviour, not of tests. Each one means building
  something in Rust first.

## Surprising

- **Several gaps are bugs, not missing tests.** Items 1 to 4 above, plus the Cursor config one, change what Locust
  does to the person's files or permissions today. They deserve fixes before anyone ports more tests.
- **One missing UI piece explains many gaps.** Rust has no hover or tooltip anywhere in `src/`. Full labels, effort
  levels on hover and blind comparison names all trace to that.
- **Rust has no thinking orb.** Four tests (orb asset, orb states, the working orb) wait on a component that is not
  planned anywhere in `rows.tsv`.
- **Markdown is an external crate.** Rust hands replies to rune-kit's markdown, which was not read. Six
  `agentText` tests (lists, bullets, nested lists, links in emphasis, tool calls as text) are `gap` because they
  could not be verified, not because they are known to fail. Read rune-kit's renderer once and most of them settle.
- **Locust in a browser acts like a missing feature without a row.** Six files (desk host, desk snapshot,
  browser settings, ElsewhereApp) all fail on the same missing `DeskHost`. A row for it in `rows.tsv` would let them
  move to `feature-missing`.
- **Voice and phone rows are a large share.** Rows 10 (24 files) and 12 (55) hold 79 of the 85 `feature-missing` files. They are
  safe to ignore until those features are scheduled.
- **The review feature is absent.** "Ask for a review", the review brief and the reviewer pair (5 files) have no Rust
  counterpart at all, and the feature is not listed as missing in `rows.tsv`.
