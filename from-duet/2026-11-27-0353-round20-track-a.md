## Row 2: El::hover_border

`hover_border(color)` sets the border's colour while hovered. It follows the same rule as `.hover`:
- the element's own `hover_motion`, else the nearest `hover_motion_within`, else landing at once and fading out over 0.1 s;
- nothing while inert, behind a modal or under a `hover_alone` child.

How it works without touching `paint/build.rs` (Speed 1's file, which paints `P_BORDER` from `vis.border`): on a hover flip the node swaps `vis.border` with the stored hover colour and animates `P_BORDER` on the flip's curve. A commit while hovered relights the fresh node from the hover track's target.

Files: `el/visual.rs`, `el/visuals.rs`, `runtime/input/hover.rs`, `runtime/input/pointer.rs`, `runtime/commit/apply.rs`, `runtime/commit/memo_reuse.rs`, `runtime/paint/tracks.rs`, `runtime/paint/cache_layer.rs`.

**Memo reuse:** a lit node and its fresh unlit rebuild compare equal through `border_pair()` (rest and hover colours, whichever is showing), so hovering doesn't defeat reuse. The apply-skip check in `paint/tracks.rs` also compares the drawn `vis.border`, which fixed a border going back to rest on a commit while hovered.

Tests (`vendor/rune/crates/rune/tests/hover_border.rs`, 5 of 5 pass):
- the_border_takes_its_hover_colour_under_the_pointer_and_rests_again_after
- it_follows_the_elements_own_hover_curves
- it_follows_hover_motion_within_from_an_ancestor
- a_commit_while_hovered_keeps_the_hover_border
- an_inert_element_keeps_its_rest_border

**No `group_border`:** the group hover factor and the border paint both live in `runtime/paint/build.rs`, which is Speed 1's. It wasn't cheap without that file.
