## Deny is `decline`

v2 takes `decline` (the turn carries on without the action) or `cancel` (also interrupts the turn). Electron's Deny means
"don't do that, carry on": the card says the reason reaches a Codex teammate "as its next input on Codex"
(ApprovalCard.tsx, DENY AND SAY WHY), and both apps send it as `turn/steer` into the running turn, which `cancel` would
have ended. The legacy `execCommandApproval`/`applyPatchApproval` (never put on a card in either app) now get the
matching legacy variant `{"denied":{"rejection":"..."}}` ("continue and try something else"); `abort` is the legacy
`cancel`.
