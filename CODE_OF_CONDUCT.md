# Code of conduct

Locust is built in the open. This repository is where people report problems,
suggest ideas and, by invitation, change the code. Everyone here is owed the
same respect.

## What we ask

- Be kind, and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific: what you saw, what you expected, what you tried.
- Help people who are new. Everyone was new to agents once.
- Leave out private things: keys, passwords, other people's personal details,
  and anything from a project that is not yours to share.

## What is not welcome

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private
  messages.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, and posts made only to advertise.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call out of place here.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and Discussions, and wherever
someone speaks for the project, such as a post from its accounts.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or discussion (its ⋯
menu), or email support@locust.lol with "Conduct" in the subject. The person
you report is not told who reported them.

## What the maintainers may do

Depending on what happened: a private word, an edited or hidden comment, a
locked thread, or a ban from the repository, for a while or for good. The
maintainers are held to this code too.

This code follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
